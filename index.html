Heads Up

Good to Know

🌡️
Weather: March highs around 38–45°F, sunny skies expected. Pack layers — mornings on the mountain are cold.
🧥
Elk Ride — DRESS WARM: The National Elk Refuge sleigh ride is open air with no blankets provided. Full ski gear or heavy winter layers recommended.
🎿
Gear tips: Helmet strongly recommended on JHMR. Hand warmers essential. Lip balm + sunscreen — altitude sun is intense on snow.
⛰️
Altitude: JHMR base is 6,311 ft, summit 10,450 ft. Stay hydrated — altitude dehydration is real, especially with alcohol.
🚑
Emergency: St. John's Medical Center · 625 E Broadway Ave · (307) 733-3636
Urgent Care: Teton Urgent Care · (307) 201-1984
🎟️
Lift Tickets: Buy in advance online at jacksonhole.com — walk-up window prices are significantly higher.
